The definitions of the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA-individual.md) apply, with these additions:

"Corporation" means the legal entity signing this agreement, together with all entities that control it, are controlled by it or are under common control with it. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting rights or the power to direct management.
