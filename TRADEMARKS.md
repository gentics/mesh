# Gentics Trademark Policy

Version 1.0

Gentics CMP is open source: the core is licensed under the Apache License 2.0, and the Enterprise Modules under the Business Source License 1.1. These licenses cover the code. They do not grant any rights to use the Gentics name or logos. This policy explains how you may use them.

We want you to talk about Gentics CMP, build on it, teach it and offer services around it. We also want users to know whether software or a service comes from Gentics. When in doubt, ask us at sales@gentics.com.

## 1. Marks covered

This policy covers the following marks of Gentics Software GmbH ("Gentics Marks"):

* the name "Gentics", a registered trademark in the European Union;
* product names containing "Gentics", such as "Gentics CMP", "Gentics CMS" and "Gentics Mesh";
* the Gentics logos.

We do not claim exclusive rights in the term "CMP" on its own. However, combining it with names or logos that suggest a connection to Gentics falls under this policy (see Section 5).

## 2. Always allowed

You may, without asking:

* refer to Gentics CMP truthfully in articles, talks, books, tutorials, courses and social media;
* state that your product, plugin or integration works with Gentics CMP, using phrases such as "for Gentics CMP", "compatible with Gentics CMP" or "integrates with Gentics CMP";
* state that you offer services such as consulting, implementation, hosting or training for Gentics CMP, as long as you do not suggest you are Gentics or a Gentics partner unless you are one;
* redistribute unmodified official releases of Gentics CMP under their original name.

Use the plain text name, not the logo, and do not make the Gentics Marks more prominent than your own name.

## 3. Allowed with conditions

* **User groups and community events** may use "Gentics CMP" in their name, e.g. "Gentics CMP User Group Vienna", if they are non-commercial and state that they are not organized by Gentics.
* **Community packages and extensions** may use "for Gentics CMP" in their description, but not "Gentics" as the first part of their name.
* **Logos** may be used in unchanged form to link to gentics.com or to the official project, e.g. in a list of supported platforms. Do not alter, animate or combine them with other marks.

## 4. Forks and modified versions

You are free to fork Gentics CMP under its licenses.

* If you publicly distribute a modified version, you must give it a name that does not contain "Gentics" and is not confusingly similar to a Gentics Mark. Remove the Gentics logos from the user interface and the documentation.
* You may state truthfully that your version is "based on Gentics CMP" or "a fork of Gentics CMP".
* Unmodified redistribution, including repackaging an official release without changes to its code, may keep the original name.
* Private modifications that you do not distribute publicly, e.g. within your organization, may keep the original name.

## 5. Not allowed

Without written permission from Gentics, you may not:

* use a Gentics Mark in the name of a product, service, company or project, such as "Gentics CMP Cloud" or "Gentics Hosting";
* offer a hosted or managed service in a way that suggests it is provided or endorsed by Gentics;
* register or use domain names, social media accounts or app names that contain a Gentics Mark or a confusingly similar term;
* use a Gentics Mark in a way that suggests Gentics sponsors, endorses or certifies you, your product or your service;
* use a Gentics Mark in a misleading, disparaging or unlawful way;
* create marks or logos that are confusingly similar to the Gentics Marks.

## 6. Permission and partners

If you want to use a Gentics Mark in a way this policy does not allow, write to sales@gentics.com. Official Gentics partners receive separate usage guidelines with their partner agreement.

## 7. Reporting misuse

If you see a use that confuses users about the origin of software or services, please let us know at sales@gentics.com.

## 8. Changes

Gentics may update this policy. The current version is published as `TRADEMARKS.md` in the [Gentics CMP repository](https://github.com/gentics/cmp) and mirrored in the other Gentics CMP repositories.
